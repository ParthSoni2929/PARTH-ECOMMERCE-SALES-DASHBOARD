# 📊 Parth Ecommerce Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a detailed analysis of ecommerce sales performance across multiple business areas such as sales, profit, customers, categories, states, and payment methods. The dashboard helps businesses make data-driven decisions through interactive visualizations and KPI tracking.

# 📈 Dashboard Insights

## 🔹 Sales by State
- Displays total sales generated from different states.
- Helps identify top-performing regions.
- Maharashtra contributes the highest sales among all states.

## 🔹 Quantity by Category
- Shows category-wise quantity distribution using a donut chart.
- Clothing category contributes the highest share (63%).
- Helps understand customer purchasing behavior.

## 🔹 Profit by Month
- Monthly profit trend visualization.
- Positive and negative profit months are clearly represented.
- November recorded the highest profit.

## 🔹 Sales by Customer
- Displays top customers based on purchase amount.
- Useful for customer analysis and retention strategies.

## 🔹 Quantity by Payment Mode
- Represents customer payment preferences.
- COD (Cash on Delivery) is the most used payment method.
- Includes UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and EMI payment analysis.

## 🔹 Profit by Sub-Category
- Shows profit generated by different product sub-categories.
- Printers and Bookcases are the most profitable products.
- Helps identify high-performing product segments.
